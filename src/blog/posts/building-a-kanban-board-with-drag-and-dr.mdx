TL;DR Drag-and-drop features are now common across modern web applications. They can be used to arrange items in order, categorize things, and perform other tasks. This makes a visual impact on the user, indicating that they are working and something is moving. Additionally, it can be helpful when multiple actions are needed to perform, making it easy to drag and drop. The Kanban Board is one of those examples where you can see the drag-and-drop of a component in its peak form. Most of the to-do lists use this Kanban board to manage the order and progress of tasks. As developers, we have seen it multiple times in JIRA boards. So, today we are going to build our kanban board that can be used at multiple places as per requirement. So, let’s get started. This kit can be divided into 4 major topics. Here are those: : This is used as the main provided that wraps the functionality of draggable and droppable.
