It is the standard language used to create and structure content on the web. Think of HTML as the skeleton of a webpage. It tells the browser how to display text, images, links, videos, and more. Why is HTML important? HTML is important because it: Structures your webpage content. Helps search engines understand your site. Works with CSS and JavaScript to make your site look good and interact with users.
