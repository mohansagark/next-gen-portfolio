🎮 Fun Fact from My AR Mini-Game Experiment! I’ve been blending Montessori-style “learning by doing” 🧩 with 3D LEGO-inspired construction 🏗️ in my AR mini-game prototype: • Players stitch shapes together to form platforms or collectible containers. • Then they stack, rotate, and combine pieces in 3D—engaging logic, spatial reasoning, and creativity simultaneously. • Subtle hints appear only when stuck, keeping the experience self-directed and exploratory 🌟. • Each level progressively increases complexity, encouraging experimentation without overwhelming the player. 💡 Why it’s exciting for me: This method bridges kinesthetic intuition and engineering-style problem solving, making gameplay a hands-on learning journey, not just a digital experience.
