How would you go about building a responsive website? (Easy) Responsive design is one of the fundamentals you’ll be asked about. Building a responsive website means your layout, images, and typography adapt gracefully to any device or screen size. The basic tools for responsive design include relative units (such as %, em, and rem), media queries, and fluid layouts. Most interviews expect you to mention a “mobile-first” approach, where your base styles are designed for mobile devices and scaled up for larger screens.
